Product Details

The PV (photovoltaic) glass cleaning equipment is a precision cleaning system engineered specifically for solar panel manufacturers. Clean, contamination-free glass is essential before applying anti-reflective (AR) and transparent conductive oxide (TCO) coatings, as even microscopic residues can cause coating defects, reduce light transmission, and lower panel efficiency. Our system uses a multi-stage cleaning process combining ultrasonic cleaning, high-pressure water jet spraying, mechanical brush scrubbing, and final pure water rinsing to remove all contaminants, ensuring optimal light transmission and superior coating adhesion.

Cleaning Process

The equipment performs a sequential multi-stage cleaning process to achieve the high cleanliness standards required for PV glass coating:

  • Ultrasonic Pre-Cleaning: Glass enters an ultrasonic cleaning tank where high-frequency cavitation bubbles penetrate and dislodge fine particulates, dust, and organic residues from the glass surface, including microscopic surface defects and edges that mechanical cleaning alone cannot reach.
  • High-Pressure Spray: Pressurized water jets spray the glass surface from multiple angles, flushing away loosened contaminants and performing a primary rinse of the bulk surface area.
  • Brush Cleaning: Soft-contact rotating brushes mechanically scrub both surfaces of the glass, removing stubborn residues and ensuring uniform cleanliness across the entire panel area without scratching or damaging the glass.
  • Pure Water Rinsing: A final rinse with high-purity deionized water (>15 MOhm.cm) removes all remaining dissolved solids and trace ions, leaving a spot-free, contamination-free surface ready for coating.
  • Hot Air Drying: Filtered hot air dries the cleaned glass completely, preventing water spots and ensuring the surface is immediately ready for the downstream coating process.

Features

Our PV glass cleaning equipment is designed to meet the demanding requirements of high-throughput solar glass production lines:

  • Multi-Stage Cleaning: The integrated five-stage process covers every cleaning need from gross contaminant removal to final spot-free rinsing, delivering consistently high cleanliness for coating preparation.
  • Pure Water Rinse: High-purity deionized water exceeding 15 MOhm.cm conductivity is used for the final rinse, guaranteeing no residual ions or minerals remain on the glass to interfere with coating adhesion.
  • Automated Operation: Conveyor-driven continuous operation with PLC control handles glass loading, cleaning, rinsing, drying, and unloading automatically, minimizing labor and ensuring repeatable process quality.
  • Adjustable Speed: Conveyor speed is adjustable from 1 to 5 m/min, allowing operators to optimize cleaning dwell time for different glass sizes, contamination levels, and production throughput requirements.
  • High Cleanliness: The combined ultrasonic, brush, and pure water process consistently achieves the ultra-clean surface required for high-quality AR and TCO coating deposition, maximizing panel light transmission and conversion efficiency.

Applications

The PV glass cleaning equipment serves manufacturers across the solar and specialty glass industries:

  • PV Glass Manufacturing: Cleaning of tempered and un-tempered photovoltaic cover glass before anti-reflective coating, the primary application for this equipment.
  • Solar Panel Production: Integration into solar module production lines to prepare glass substrates for thin-film deposition and lamination processes.
  • Optical Glass Cleaning: Cleaning of precision optical glass components for lenses, mirrors, and laser optics where surface cleanliness is critical to optical performance.
  • Electronic Glass Cleaning: Cleaning of glass substrates used in electronic displays, touch panels, and semiconductor applications where particulate and ionic contamination must be strictly controlled.
