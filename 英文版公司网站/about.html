Company Profile

Wuhan Hengtongyuan Environmental Engineering Technology Co., Ltd., established in 2008, is a technology enterprise integrating R&D, manufacturing, installation, commissioning, and operation management of water treatment equipment and environmental protection equipment. The company has its own production factory, over 60 employees, 5 master's degree holders in environmental engineering, 1 senior engineer, and 6 intermediate engineers.

The company holds Level II qualifications for environmental protection engineering, municipal public works general contracting, and mechanical and electrical equipment installation engineering. In 2015, the company was successfully listed on the Shanghai Equity Exchange Center, name: Hengtongyuan, code: 205308.

Business Scope:

- Integrated sewage treatment equipment

- Electroplating wastewater treatment equipment

- RO (reverse osmosis) water purification systems

- VOC exhaust gas treatment systems

- Rainwater utilization and recovery equipment

- Cyclone dust removal systems

- PV glass cleaning equipment

Our History

Founded in 2008, Wuhan Hengtongyuan Environmental Engineering Technology Co., Ltd. has grown from a small team into a recognized environmental engineering enterprise in central China. Over the years, we have served numerous government agencies, schools, hospitals, industrial parks, and enterprises across the country.

In 2015, the company achieved a major milestone by being listed on the Shanghai Equity Exchange Center (stock code: 205308), marking our commitment to transparency and corporate governance.

Our Team

Our team consists of over 60 dedicated professionals, including 5 master's degree holders in environmental engineering, 1 senior engineer, and 6 intermediate engineers. With strong technical expertise and years of practical experience, we provide comprehensive solutions tailored to each client's needs.

Our Qualifications

We hold Level II qualifications in three key areas:

- Environmental protection engineering

- Municipal public works general contracting

- Mechanical and electrical equipment installation engineering

These qualifications reflect our technical capabilities and commitment to quality standards in every project we undertake.
