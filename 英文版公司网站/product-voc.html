VOC Exhaust Gas Treatment

The VOC exhaust gas treatment system is designed for chemical plants, coating workshops, printing facilities, and other industries generating volatile organic compounds. Using a combination of activated carbon adsorption, catalytic combustion, and biological treatment technologies, the system achieves VOC removal rates of over 90-95%, ensuring compliance with national and local emission standards.

Product Details

Working Principle

The VOC exhaust gas treatment system employs a multi-stage process to effectively remove volatile organic compounds from industrial exhaust gas:

  • Activated Carbon Adsorption: The exhaust gas first passes through activated carbon beds, where VOC molecules are physically adsorbed onto the porous carbon surface. This stage captures the majority of organic compounds and reduces overall VOC concentration.
  • Catalytic Oxidation (CO): For moderate to high VOC concentrations, catalytic combustion is employed. The gas is heated to 250-350°C and passed over a catalyst bed, where VOCs are oxidized into carbon dioxide and water vapor. The catalyst lowers the activation energy, allowing complete oxidation at lower temperatures than thermal incineration.
  • Biological Treatment: For low-concentration, mixed VOC streams, biological filters use microorganisms to metabolize organic compounds into harmless byproducts. Biofilters are especially effective for odorous and biodegradable VOCs.

Features

  • High Efficiency: Achieves VOC removal rates exceeding 90-95%, meeting national and local emission standards for a wide range of organic pollutants.
  • Low Energy Consumption: Catalytic oxidation operates at significantly lower temperatures than thermal incineration, reducing fuel consumption and operating costs. Heat recovery systems further improve energy efficiency.
  • Compact Design: The integrated modular design minimizes the equipment footprint, making it suitable for facilities with limited installation space. Modules can be combined for higher flow rates.
  • Automated Operation: A PLC-based control system monitors temperature, pressure, and concentration in real time, enabling unattended operation with automatic start/stop and alarm functions.

Applications

  • Chemical Industry: Petrochemical plants, chemical synthesis, solvent production, and chemical storage and transfer operations.
  • Printing: Gravure and flexographic printing, coating and laminating processes in packaging manufacturing.
  • Coating and Surface Treatment: Automotive coating lines, furniture spray booths, metal coating, and dip-coating operations.
  • Electronics: Semiconductor manufacturing, PCB production, and electronic component cleaning processes.
  • Pharmaceuticals: Drug manufacturing, solvent recovery, and chemical synthesis exhaust gas treatment.
