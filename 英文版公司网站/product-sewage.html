Product Details

Treatment Process

The integrated sewage treatment system employs a multi-stage biological and physical treatment process:

  • Primary Sedimentation: Raw sewage enters a primary sedimentation tank where settleable solids, suspended matter, and floating debris are removed by gravity settling. This stage reduces the organic load on downstream biological treatment and protects biological process units from solids overload.
  • Biological Treatment: The core biological stage uses advanced processes selected based on influent characteristics and discharge requirements:
    • A/O (Anoxic/Oxic): An anaerobic/anoxic zone followed by an aerobic zone for nitrogen removal via nitrification and denitrification.
    • A2/O (Anaerobic-Anoxic-Oxic): A three-stage process achieving simultaneous nitrogen and phosphorus removal through polyphosphate-accumulating organisms (PAOs).
    • MBR (Membrane Bioreactor): Combines biological treatment with membrane filtration, achieving superior effluent quality and higher biomass concentration in a smaller footprint.
  • Secondary Sedimentation: The biological mixed liquor flows to a secondary clarifier where activated sludge settles and is partially returned to the biological reactor. Excess sludge is removed for separate treatment and disposal.
  • Disinfection: The final effluent undergoes UV or chlorine disinfection to eliminate pathogenic microorganisms before discharge or reuse, ensuring the water meets sanitary standards for safe environmental release or non-potable reuse.

Features

  • Compact Design: The integrated, factory-assembled equipment combines all treatment stages within a single skid or containerized unit, minimizing the on-site footprint and eliminating the need for extensive civil construction.
  • Low Energy Consumption: High-efficiency aeration systems with fine-bubble diffusers and variable-frequency blowers optimize oxygen transfer while reducing power consumption by 20-30% compared to conventional systems.
  • Stable Performance: The biological process maintains consistent treatment efficiency across varying influent flow and loading conditions, with robust resistance to shock loads and seasonal temperature variations.
  • Easy Operation: The PLC-based control system enables fully automated operation with remote monitoring capability. Parameters such as dissolved oxygen, pH, and flow are continuously monitored and automatically adjusted.
  • Minimal Odor: Enclosed treatment zones with odor collection and treatment systems ensure that surrounding areas remain free from unpleasant odors, making the system suitable for deployment near residential areas.

Applications

  • Residential Communities: Small to medium-sized housing developments, apartment complexes, and residential neighborhoods requiring on-site sewage treatment.
  • Schools and Universities: Campus wastewater treatment for educational institutions, dormitories, and cafeteria waste streams.
  • Hotels: Hotel and resort complexes requiring reliable on-site wastewater treatment and potential water reuse for landscaping.
  • Hospitals: Medical facility wastewater treatment with disinfection to meet hospital sewage discharge standards.
  • Industrial Parks: Light industrial zones, office parks, and manufacturing campuses requiring centralized wastewater treatment.
  • Rural Sewage Treatment: Village-scale decentralized sewage treatment for rural communities without access to centralized municipal sewer systems.
