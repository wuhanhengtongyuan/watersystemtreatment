Product Details

Working Principle

Reverse osmosis is a pressure-driven membrane separation process that removes contaminants at the molecular level:

  • Pressure-Driven Membrane Separation: A high-pressure pump forces feed water through a semi-permeable membrane with a pore size of approximately 0.0001 microns. Water molecules pass through while dissolved salts, organic compounds, bacteria, viruses, and colloidal particles are rejected and concentrated in the reject stream (brine).
  • 0.0001 Micron Pore Size: The extremely fine pore size of the RO membrane allows only water molecules and a very limited number of low-molecular-weight ions to pass through. This achieves rejection rates of 97-99% for dissolved solids, effectively removing heavy metals (lead, arsenic, chromium, mercury), bacteria, viruses, pesticides, and organic contaminants.
  • Concentrate and Permeate Streams: The feed water is split into two streams: the permeate (purified water that passes through the membrane) and the concentrate (rejected contaminants in the brine stream). The ratio of permeate to feed water is the system's recovery rate.

Features

  • High Rejection Rate: Achieves 97-99% desalination rate, removing virtually all dissolved solids, microorganisms, and organic pollutants for ultra-pure water production.
  • Energy-Efficient: Modern low-energy membranes and energy recovery devices reduce power consumption. High-pressure pumps with variable frequency drives (VFD) optimize energy use across varying flow conditions.
  • Automatic Control: PLC-based control system monitors inlet pressure, membrane differential pressure, flow rates, conductivity, and pH in real time. Automatic flush, CIP (clean-in-place), and alarm functions ensure unattended reliable operation.
  • Modular Design: Standardized membrane housings and skid-mounted frames allow capacity expansion by adding parallel membrane elements. The modular approach simplifies maintenance, as individual membrane elements can be replaced without system shutdown.

Applications

  • Power Plant Water: Boiler make-up water and cooling tower make-up water for thermal and nuclear power plants, requiring low-conductivity demineralized water.
  • Pharmaceutical Pure Water: Purified water (PW) and water for injection (WFI) pretreatment systems for pharmaceutical manufacturing, complying with pharmacopeia standards.
  • Food & Beverage Process Water: Ingredient water for beverage production, dairy processing, brewery and distillery operations, and food processing requiring consistent water quality.
  • Electroplating Wastewater Recovery: Recovery of heavy metal-bearing rinse water from electroplating and surface treatment operations, enabling closed-loop water recycling and metal reclamation.
