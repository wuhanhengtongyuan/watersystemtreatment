What is the Price of Integrated Sewage Treatment Equipment in Wuhan?

Author: Admin Published: Jun 11, 2021 Category: Company News Views: 986

What is the price of integrated sewage treatment equipment? This is a common question from customers looking to invest in water treatment solutions. The price of integrated sewage treatment equipment varies significantly depending on several key factors.

First, let's understand the installation requirements. If the equipment is placed above ground, a concrete foundation matching the equipment shape is required. The foundation is generally 200mm higher than the ground level to prevent rainwater from entering. If installed underground, excavation and concrete pit construction are needed, which adds to the total project cost.

Factors affecting the price of integrated sewage treatment equipment include:

1. Treatment Capacity: The daily processing capacity (tons/day) is the primary factor. Small units (5-50 tons/day) are less expensive, while larger systems (100-500 tons/day) require more materials and components.

2. Influent Water Quality: The quality of incoming sewage determines the complexity of the treatment process. Higher pollutant concentrations may require additional treatment stages, increasing the equipment cost.

3. Effluent Standards: Meeting Class I-B standards requires less equipment than meeting Class I-A standards. Stricter discharge requirements mean more advanced treatment processes and higher costs.

4. Treatment Process: Different biological processes (A/O, A2/O, MBR, SBR) have different equipment configurations and price points. MBR systems, for example, cost more due to membrane modules.

5. Material Quality: The choice of materials for the equipment body (carbon steel, stainless steel, FRP) affects both price and longevity. Stainless steel is the most expensive but offers the best corrosion resistance.

6. Automation Level: Higher levels of automation (PLC control, remote monitoring, automatic dosing) increase the equipment price but reduce labor costs.

7. Site Conditions: Installation site accessibility, space constraints, and site preparation requirements affect the total project cost.
