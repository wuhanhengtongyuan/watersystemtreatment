About Us

Wuhan Hengtongyuan Environmental Engineering Technology Co., Ltd., established in 2008, is a technology enterprise integrating R&D, manufacturing, installation, commissioning, and operation management of water treatment equipment and environmental protection equipment. The company has its own production factory, over 60 employees, 5 master's degree holders in environmental engineering, 1 senior engineer, and 6 intermediate engineers. The company holds Level II qualifications for environmental protection engineering, municipal public works general contracting, and mechanical and electrical equipment installation engineering. In 2015, the company was successfully listed on the Shanghai Equity Exchange Center, name: Hengtongyuan, code: 205308.
