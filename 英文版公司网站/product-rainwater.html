Product Details

The rainwater utilization system is an integrated solution designed to collect, filter, store, and treat rainwater from rooftops and ground surfaces for non-potable applications. By capturing and reusing stormwater that would otherwise be lost to runoff, the system helps conserve precious tap water resources and reduces the burden on municipal drainage infrastructure.

System Components

The rainwater utilization system is composed of several integrated modules working together to deliver clean, reusable water:

  • Collection System: Rooftop gutters, downspouts, and surface drainage channels that gather rainwater from catchment areas and direct it to the treatment unit.
  • Initial Rainwater Discarding Device: Automatically diverts the first flush of rainfall, which carries the heaviest contamination from roof and ground surfaces, away from the storage tank to protect water quality.
  • Filtration System: Multi-stage filters including screen filters and sand filters that remove leaves, sediment, suspended solids, and other particulates from the collected rainwater.
  • Storage Tank: Modular storage tanks made of HDPE, fiberglass, or stainless steel that hold the filtered rainwater. Tanks are available in sizes from 5 to 500 cubic meters depending on project requirements.
  • Disinfection Unit: Ultraviolet (UV) sterilizers or chlorination systems that eliminate bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens, ensuring the stored water meets hygiene standards for non-potable use.
  • Distribution Pump: Pressurized pumping system that delivers treated rainwater to end-use points such as irrigation systems, toilet flushing, and cleaning stations with consistent flow and pressure.

Features

Our rainwater utilization equipment is engineered for long-term reliable operation with the following key features:

  • Automatic Control: PLC-based control system with sensors monitors water levels, flow rates, and water quality, automating the entire treatment and distribution process with minimal operator intervention.
  • Low Maintenance: Durable, corrosion-resistant components and self-cleaning filter designs reduce maintenance frequency and operating costs over the system's long service life.
  • Modular Design: The system is built from standardized modules that can be easily expanded, reconfigured, or relocated as project demands change, offering outstanding flexibility for different site conditions.
  • Significant Water Savings: By substituting rainwater for tap water in non-potable applications, the system reduces municipal water consumption by 30-50%, delivering rapid return on investment.

Applications

The rainwater utilization system is suitable for a wide range of facilities where non-potable water demand is high and roof or surface catchment area is available:

  • Schools: Toilet flushing, campus landscape irrigation, and sports field watering.
  • Parks: Lawn and garden irrigation, ornamental pond replenishment, and pathway cleaning.
  • Residential Communities: Landscape watering, community cleaning, and toilet flushing in common areas.
  • Industrial Facilities: Process make-up water, cooling tower supply, equipment washing, and site cleaning.
  • Commercial Buildings: Toilet flushing, landscape irrigation, and evaporative cooling make-up water for office complexes and shopping centers.
