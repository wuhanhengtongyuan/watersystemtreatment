Resource Utilization of Urban Sewage Helps Improve Water Quality

Author: Admin Published: Apr 21, 2018 Category: Industry News Views: 634

Resource utilization of urban sewage can increase available water resources and reduce pollution, which is beneficial for improving water quality. After treatment, urban sewage can be used for industrial cooling, urban greening, road cleaning, toilet flushing, and other non-potable applications, effectively reducing the demand for fresh water.

With rapid urbanization and population growth, water scarcity has become an increasingly serious issue in many cities. Urban sewage, if properly treated, represents a significant alternative water resource. The concept of "turning waste into resources" has gained recognition among policymakers, environmental engineers, and water utilities.

The main pathways for urban sewage resource utilization include:

1. Industrial Reuse: Treated urban sewage can be used as industrial cooling water, boiler feed water (after further treatment), or process water. Many power plants, steel mills, and chemical factories have successfully adopted reclaimed water for their cooling systems.

2. Urban Non-Potable Use: Reclaimed water is suitable for urban landscape irrigation, park maintenance, road cleaning, and fire protection. These applications consume large volumes of water that would otherwise come from potable water supplies.

3. Building and Residential Use: Treated sewage can be used for toilet flushing in residential buildings, commercial complexes, and public facilities. This dual-water-supply system significantly reduces domestic tap water consumption.

4. Agricultural Irrigation: In some regions, properly treated urban sewage meets agricultural irrigation water standards, providing both water and nutrients for crops while reducing fertilizer usage.

5. Ecological Replenishment: Treated urban sewage can be discharged into rivers, lakes, and wetlands to replenish ecological water, maintaining water body health and improving the surrounding environment.

The key to successful urban sewage resource utilization lies in appropriate treatment technology and water quality management. Advanced treatment processes such as membrane bioreactor (MBR), reverse osmosis (RO), and advanced oxidation can produce high-quality reclaimed water meeting various reuse standards.
