Product Details

The high-salinity food wastewater treatment system is a specialized solution engineered for food processing plants that generate wastewater containing high concentrations of dissolved salts. Traditional wastewater treatment processes struggle with high-salinity streams, as elevated salt levels inhibit conventional biological treatment. Our system overcomes this challenge by combining salt-tolerant biological treatment, advanced membrane separation, and evaporation crystallization technologies to effectively remove COD, BOD, SS, and dissolved salts, achieving compliance with stringent discharge standards and supporting zero liquid discharge (ZLD) objectives.

Treatment Process

The system employs a multi-stage treatment train designed to handle the complex composition of high-salinity food wastewater:

  • Screening: Mechanical screens and rotary drum filters remove large solids, food debris, and packaging fragments from the raw wastewater to protect downstream equipment.
  • Grease Removal: Dissolved air flotation (DAF) and grease traps separate oils, fats, and grease (FOG) that are common in food processing wastewater, preventing downstream fouling.
  • Biological Treatment: Salt-tolerant (halophilic) bacteria in an MBR (membrane bioreactor) or activated sludge system degrade organic pollutants (COD and BOD) under high-salinity conditions, with carefully acclimated microbial consortia that thrive in saline environments.
  • RO Membrane: Reverse osmosis membranes concentrate dissolved salts and remaining organics, producing a high-quality permeate for reuse or discharge and a concentrated brine stream for further treatment.
  • Evaporation Crystallization: The concentrated brine is processed through mechanical vapor recompression (MVR) or multi-effect evaporation followed by crystallization, recovering solid salt for disposal or reuse and achieving zero liquid discharge.

Features

Our high-salinity food wastewater system delivers reliable, high-performance treatment with the following features:

  • Salt-Tolerant Bacteria: Specially acclimated halophilic microbial cultures maintain high biological activity and treatment performance even at salt concentrations of 1-5%, where conventional biological processes fail.
  • High COD Removal: The combined biological and membrane treatment achieves COD removal exceeding 95%, meeting strict discharge standards for food industry effluent.
  • ZLD Capable: Integrated evaporation crystallization enables zero liquid discharge, eliminating wastewater discharge entirely and recovering clean water for reuse and solid salt for safe disposal or recycling.
  • Compact Design: Skid-mounted and modular equipment layout minimizes the system footprint, making it suitable for food plants with limited space available for wastewater treatment.
  • Automatic Control: PLC and SCADA-based automation continuously monitors and adjusts process parameters including pH, dissolved oxygen, flow rates, and membrane pressure, ensuring stable operation with minimal operator attention.

Applications

The system is designed for food processing facilities that produce high-salinity wastewater typical of these industry segments:

  • Pickled Food Processing: Pickles, kimchi, and fermented vegetable production generates high-salt brine wastewater requiring specialized treatment.
  • Sauce Production: Soy sauce, fish sauce, and other condiment manufacturing produces wastewater with high salinity and high organic content.
  • Seafood Processing: Shrimp, fish, and shellfish processing generates saline wastewater from brining, cooking, and cleaning operations.
  • Dairy Industry: Cheese production and dairy processing generate wastewater with high salinity from brine curing and equipment cleaning.
  • Snack Food Manufacturing: Salty snack production including chips, crackers, and processed nuts generates saline, high-COD wastewater.
