Product Details

Filtration Process

The water purification system employs a comprehensive multi-stage treatment process to ensure the highest quality of output water:

  • Sand Filtration: Raw water passes through multi-media sand filters that remove suspended solids, sediment, colloidal particles, and large particulate matter. This stage protects downstream membrane components from fouling and extends their service life.
  • Activated Carbon Filtration: Granular activated carbon adsorbs organic compounds, residual chlorine, chloramines, color, odor, and taste-causing substances. This stage improves water aesthetics and protects the RO membrane from chlorine damage.
  • RO Membrane Filtration: The reverse osmosis membrane removes dissolved solids, heavy metals (lead, arsenic, cadmium), bacteria, viruses, and organic molecules at the 0.0001-micron level. This is the core purification stage, achieving TDS reduction of up to 98%.
  • UV Sterilization: Final ultraviolet disinfection (254 nm wavelength) destroys the DNA of any remaining bacteria and viruses, providing a chemical-free final barrier against microbial contamination.

Features

  • Multi-Stage Treatment: A four-stage process of sand filtration, carbon adsorption, RO membrane separation, and UV sterilization ensures comprehensive removal of physical, chemical, and biological contaminants.
  • Compact Design: Skid-mounted, integrated design minimizes the equipment footprint. All components are pre-assembled and factory-tested, reducing on-site installation time and complexity.
  • Low Maintenance: Modular cartridge and membrane design allows quick and easy replacement of consumable components. Sand and carbon filters can be backwashed automatically, and RO membranes are cleanable.
  • Automatic Control: A PLC controller manages the entire treatment process including filtration, backwashing, RO operation, and UV activation. The system operates unattended with automatic monitoring and alarm functions.

Applications

  • Drinking Water Supply: Residential communities, schools, office buildings, and public water stations requiring safe, high-quality drinking water.
  • Food & Beverage Industry: Bottled water production, beverage manufacturing, dairy processing, and food processing requiring purified ingredient water.
  • Pharmaceutical Industry: Pharmaceutical manufacturing, laboratory water, and medical facility water systems meeting purified water and WFI standards.
  • Electronics Manufacturing: Semiconductor fabrication, PCB production, and precision electronics cleaning requiring ultra-pure process water.
