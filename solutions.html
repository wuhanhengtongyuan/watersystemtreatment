Our Solutions

Sewage Treatment Solutions

Our integrated sewage treatment equipment is designed for residential communities, schools, hotels, hospitals, and industrial parks. Using advanced biological treatment processes, the system effectively removes COD, BOD, ammonia nitrogen, and other pollutants. The equipment features compact design, low energy consumption, easy operation, and stable performance. It can be installed above ground or underground, making it suitable for various site conditions. Treatment capacity ranges from 5 to 500 tons per day, meeting Class I-A discharge standards.

Industrial Wastewater Treatment Solutions

We provide specialized wastewater treatment solutions for electroplating, chemical, food processing, and other industries. Our systems use physical, chemical, and biological treatment processes tailored to specific wastewater characteristics. Key technologies include coagulation, sedimentation, filtration, reverse osmosis, and evaporation crystallization. We help enterprises achieve zero liquid discharge (ZLD) or meet stringent industry-specific discharge standards, ensuring compliance while optimizing operational costs.

Rainwater Recovery Solutions

Our rainwater collection and utilization systems help buildings and communities reduce tap water consumption by 30-50%. The system includes rainwater collection, initial filtration, storage, treatment, and distribution modules. Treated rainwater can be used for landscape irrigation, toilet flushing, road cleaning, and cooling water supply. This solution is particularly suitable for schools, parks, residential areas, and industrial facilities looking to reduce water costs and promote sustainability.

VOC Exhaust Gas Treatment Solutions

Our VOC (Volatile Organic Compounds) exhaust gas treatment systems are designed for chemical plants, printing facilities, coating workshops, and other industries generating organic exhaust gases. The solution combines multiple technologies including activated carbon adsorption, catalytic combustion, and biological treatment, achieving removal rates of over 90-95%. Systems are customizable based on exhaust volume, concentration, and composition, ensuring compliance with national and local emission standards.

Water Purification Solutions

We provide comprehensive water purification solutions using multi-stage filtration, reverse osmosis (RO), and ultraviolet sterilization technologies. Our systems can produce purified water meeting various standards, from drinking water to ultra-pure water for industrial and laboratory use. Suitable for residential, commercial, and industrial applications, our water purification equipment ensures safe, clean water supply with minimal maintenance requirements.

Dust Removal Solutions

Our cyclone dust removal systems are designed for cement plants, power stations, metallurgical facilities, and other industries generating particulate emissions. Using centrifugal force and gravity separation principles, the system effectively removes dust particles of various sizes from exhaust gases. The equipment features high efficiency, low pressure drop, easy maintenance, and durable construction, making it a cost-effective solution for industrial dust control.
