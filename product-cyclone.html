Product Details

The cyclone dust removal system is a robust and reliable air pollution control device that uses centrifugal force to separate dust and particulate matter from industrial exhaust gas streams. With no moving parts and no filter media to replace, cyclones are one of the most cost-effective and durable solutions for primary particulate removal across a wide range of heavy industries.

Working Principle

The cyclone separator operates on a straightforward principle of centrifugal separation:

  • Tangential Gas Entry: Dust-laden gas enters the cyclone chamber tangentially at the upper section, which immediately imparts a powerful rotational (swirling) motion to the gas stream.
  • Centrifugal Separation: As the gas spirals downward in a helical pattern, the centrifugal force generated by the rotational flow throws the heavier dust particles outward toward the cyclone wall. The particles lose momentum, slide down the wall, and collect in the hopper at the bottom.
  • Clean Gas Exit: The cleaned gas reverses direction at the bottom of the cyclone and spirals upward through the inner vortex, exiting through the top outlet pipe, while collected dust is discharged from the bottom hopper.

Features

Our cyclone dust removal system offers the following key advantages for industrial dust control applications:

  • High Removal Efficiency for Particles >5 Microns: The cyclone achieves 85-95% capture efficiency for particles larger than 5 microns, making it an effective primary dust collector or pre-cleaner ahead of baghouses or scrubbers.
  • Low Pressure Drop: Optimized cyclone geometry and flow paths maintain a moderate pressure drop of 800-1500 Pa, reducing fan energy consumption and operating costs.
  • Wear-Resistant Construction: Internal surfaces are lined with abrasion-resistant materials such as wear-resistant steel, ceramics, or polyurethane, extending service life in high-dust and abrasive applications.
  • Simple Operation: With no moving internal parts and no filter bags or cartridges to replace, the cyclone operates reliably with minimal supervision and very low maintenance requirements.
  • No Filter Media Needed: Unlike bag filters or cartridge collectors, the cyclone requires no filter media, eliminating the ongoing cost and downtime associated with filter replacement and disposal.

Applications

The cyclone dust removal system is widely deployed in industries that generate significant particulate emissions:

  • Cement Industry: Clinker cooler exhaust, raw mill ventilation, cement grinding, and material handling dust control.
  • Power Plants: Pre-collection of fly ash from boiler exhaust before electrostatic precipitators or baghouses.
  • Steel Mills: Dust collection from sintering, blast furnace gas cleaning, and metal scrap processing operations.
  • Mining: Crushing, screening, conveyor transfer points, and material drying exhaust dedusting.
  • Food Processing: Recovery of valuable dry product from pneumatic conveying and drying exhaust streams, and general dust control in grain and powder handling.
